ADAPTATIONS
This is a compact selection, not the complete original libraries.
Raw WAV files are mixed to mono, resampled to 44.1 kHz where necessary,
trimmed, level-matched and encoded as 16-bit PCM. Sustained recordings
receive a crossfaded loop in their held body. Short articulations use
recorded staccato/spiccato samples; available alternate takes rotate.
FluidR3 MP3 notes are extracted unchanged from the distributed data;
the player level-matches them and loops the English horn's held body.
The player transposes the nearest pitch anchor, applies velocity,
stereo seating and an envelope following the agent's note duration.

Bass clarinet uses adapted clarinet recordings; contrabassoon uses
adapted bassoon recordings; bass trombone uses tenor trombone recordings.
These are approximations, not separate recordings of those instruments.
Extreme notes beyond the recorded range are pitch-shifted. Legato uses
overlapping sustained samples, not recorded note-to-note transitions.

PROVENANCE / REBUILD
provenance.json lists every selected source path, pinned source revision,
output SHA-256 and (for converted WAVs) original file SHA-256.
scripts/build-sample-bank.py rebuilds the selection from those sources.
No samples are fetched from a third-party service during playback.
